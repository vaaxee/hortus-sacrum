Raccolta 1910 n.300 Seven Sorrows and Seven Joys of St Joseph. Indulg: 100 days once a day; 300 days every Wednesday and each day of the two novenas (19 March, Patronage); plenary on those two feasts (I,II); plenary once a month (I,II); 300 days on first six of seven consecutive Sundays; plenary on each of the seven Sundays (I-IV). Pius VII Card Vic 9 Dec 1819; Gregory XVI 22 Jan 1836; Pius IX 1 Feb & 22 Mar 1847.
[Full English texts I-VII + Ant "Jesus Himself was about thirty years old, being as was supposed the son of Joseph"; V Pray for us holy Joseph R That we may be made worthy...; Oremus: O God who in thine ineffable providence didst vouchsafe to choose blessed Joseph to be the spouse of thy most holy Mother; grant we beseech Thee that we may have him for our intercessor in heaven whom on earth we venerate as our holy Protector. Who livest...]
Prayer for agonisants n.153: 100 days each time; plenary monthly if said thrice daily at three intervals (I-IV). Pius IX 2 Feb 1850.
Ave Maris Stella n.213: 300 days once a day, Leo XIII 27 Jan 1888.
